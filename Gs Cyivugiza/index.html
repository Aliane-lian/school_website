About Cyivugiza Primary school

Welcome to GS Cyivugiza, a vibrant learning community in Nyamirambo, Kigali. With over 2,700 students, we are more than just a school, we are a family committed to nurturing young minds and shaping future leaders.

For years, we have provided quality education, embracing both tradition and innovation. While Kinyarwanda and French have historically been part of our curriculum, we continually adapt to give our students the best learning experience. Beyond academics, we believe in shaping well-rounded individuals through community initiatives and partnerships, such as working with the Rwanda Girl Guides Association to promote awareness and social impact.

At GS Cyivugiza, we inspire, empower, and prepare students for a brighter future.
